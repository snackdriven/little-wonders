A platform intended to bring tasks, habits, moods, journaling and calendar events together. Its README gives particular space to product requirements and implementation specifications, with separate plans for notifications, analytics and authentication.

This exhibit preserves the act of thinking a system through. There is a particular kind of ambition in wanting the pieces to agree with each other before asking a person to live among them.

The museum offers no verdict on whether the project was finished. The documentation is the artifact on display, rather than evidence of a particular ending.
