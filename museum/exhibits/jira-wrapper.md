A single-user viewer for saved Jira filters. The README describes five display modes, a ticket detail view and local caching, with React, TypeScript and Vite underneath.

The project does not propose eliminating the queue. It proposes choosing how the queue occupies the screen. The museum considers this a modest, specific and deeply understandable ambition.
