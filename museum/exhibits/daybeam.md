Daybeam's README proposes a productivity application with progressive disclosure: show enough to act, then reveal more when needed. The documented design brings tasks, habits, moods, journals and a calendar into the same place.

Its plans also make room for concerts, chores and countdowns. The museum finds this a persuasive definition of life: some obligations, some maintenance, and something worth looking forward to.
