Someday is the prize of the cryogenics wing: an idea kept at absolute zero, so
that it never has to meet the world that would ruin it.

It has no code. It has never had code. What it has is a domain you renew every
year, a one-line description that still gives you a little jolt, and the
unshakeable certainty that you'll build it once things calm down. Things have
not calmed down since 2019.

We do not consider Someday a failure. A failure requires an attempt. Someday is
something rarer and quieter: a perfect, frozen *maybe*, preserved at the precise
temperature where hope cannot decay and effort cannot begin. Please do not
breathe on the glass. It is closer to thawing than it looks.
