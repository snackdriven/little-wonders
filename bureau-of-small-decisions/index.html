The Bureau accepts minor, low-stakes dilemmas for formal review. Each submission is routed through the Bureau's specialist departments and returned to you with a complete record of its processing. The route a given case takes is determined during processing and is not published in advance. Most people could settle these in about two seconds. The Bureau prefers to take the matter seriously.

Why five departments?

The Bureau grew by subdivision over several decades. Each department maintains its own records, its own stamps, and its own view of the case. Coordination between them is handled by envelope.

Who is consulted?

Depending on the case: the houseplants, the Moon, the Coin Flip Division, and the Department of Vibes. All findings are recorded in the file. Weighting of findings is an internal matter.

What if I submit twice?

Repeat submissions are welcomed and processed with the same rigour as the first. The Bureau prides itself on consistency of service across all submissions, however many times a case circulates.
